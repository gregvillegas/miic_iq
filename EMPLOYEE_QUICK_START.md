# MIICiQ — Employee Quick-Start Guide

**Welcome to MIICiQ!**  
Your private internal AI assistant for company policies, Sales Order workflows, and operational procedures.

---

## 💬 1. Using Chat Mode

1. Open **MIICiQ** in your browser or intranet portal.
2. In the message box at the bottom, type your question in natural language and press **Enter**:
   - *"How do I process a Sales Order in ERP?"*
   - *"What is our annual leave rollover policy?"*
   - *"What are the meal allowances for business trips to New York?"*
   - *"What are the password requirements and 2FA policy?"*
3. **Streaming Answer:** MIICiQ will stream a grounded response step-by-step.
4. **Verified Source Citations:** Under each answer, you will see citation pills (e.g., `📎 SO_Process_Workflow_SOP_v2.docx (p.1)`). Click any pill to view the exact excerpt from the official policy document.
5. **Feedback:** Click **👍 Helpful** or **👎 Report issue** to help our knowledge base team continuously improve answer quality.

---

## 🔍 2. Using Search Mode

Need to find specific document passages or verify an SOP code directly?
1. Click **"🔍 Search Mode"** in the sidebar.
2. Enter your search keywords (e.g., `SO-101`, `discount matrix`, `per diem rates`).
3. Filter by category pills: `Policy`, `Sales Order Workflow`, `HR`, `IT`, `Finance`.
4. Review ranked passages with relevance scores, page numbers, and departmental ownership.
5. Click **"🤖 Ask MIICiQ about this passage"** to jump straight into chat with that context pre-loaded!

---

## 🛡️ 3. Privacy & Security Commitment

- **100% Private & On-Premise:** Your questions and company documents are never transmitted to external cloud providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, or external APIs).
- All AI processing occurs securely on internal company servers.
- Role-based permissions guarantee you only see documents authorized for your role and department.
